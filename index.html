Simple Plant Care

Help Your Plants Bloom

A little care every day can keep your plants beautiful and healthy.

☀️

Right Sunlight

Place your plant where it receives suitable indirect sunlight according to its variety.

💧

Proper Watering

Water when the soil feels slightly dry. Avoid keeping the roots constantly soaked.

🪴

Good Soil

Use well-draining soil and a pot with drainage holes to support healthy root growth.
